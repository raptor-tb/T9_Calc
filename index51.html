Instructions

  1. Find your leadership
  2. Select the device layout you are using (PC or Phone)
    Each device has a different order showing the troops. This option helps to match that order, making easier entering the correct numbers.
  3. Select the stacking mode
    • Conservative - guarantees the correct order of attack
    • Aggressive - allows to carry more troops with the same leadership but even a minor problem with the health bonus (missing research, wrong gem or artifact) can mess up the order of attack
    • Moderate is the middle ground
    Experiment and see what works for you
  4. Adjust the 'base number' until the total leadership in the calculator matches your leadership.
  5. Have fun 😊
